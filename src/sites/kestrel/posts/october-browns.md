Flows dropped to about 380 cfs this week and the water is as clear as it gets all year. Mornings are cold, in the low 30s at the put-in, so there's no rush to be on the water at dawn.

## What's working

- **Streamers** through the deeper runs from noon on. Olive and tan, stripped fast.
- **Blue-winged olives** on cloudy afternoons. Size 18–20, long leaders.
- **Midges** in the slow water below the bridge if nothing else is going.

The browns are starting to move upstream. Please give the gravel beds a wide berth and keep your feet out of the shallow riffles.
