The river settled at about 900 cfs after runoff, and it's fishing well from the upper canyon all the way to the take-out.

## What's working

- **Elk hair caddis** from 6pm to dark. Size 14–16.
- **PMD nymphs** under an indicator in the morning.
- **Hoppers** along the grassy banks on windy afternoons.

Water temperatures are climbing, so we're ending half days by 1pm on hot days to keep the fish healthy.
