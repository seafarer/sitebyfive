It's tempting to pitch storage as a generator replacement. On most remote sites it isn't, and pretending otherwise leads to oversized, overpriced systems.

## Diesel generators hate light loads

A generator sized for peak demand spends most of its life running at 20–30% load. That's where it's least efficient and where wet stacking shortens its life.

## Let the battery carry the valleys

With an LC-80 in the loop, the generator runs only when it can run near its sweet spot, charging the battery as it goes. Then it shuts off and the battery carries the site.

On a typical construction site that halves runtime. The generator lasts longer, the crew hauls less fuel, and nobody had to bet the project on solar.
