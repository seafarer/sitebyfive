We get asked what a pilot really involves. Here's the honest version.

## Weeks 0–2: the boring part that matters

We ask for interval load data, site photos, and access constraints. Most of the work is agreeing on **three metrics** we'll be judged on. Usually fuel displaced, runtime during an outage, and uptime.

## Week 3: deployment

Delivery, commissioning, and training typically take two days on site. The crew operates the unit from day one.

## Weeks 4–12: monitoring

We watch quietly and call when something looks odd. Most sites need one or two configuration tweaks.

## Week 13: closeout

You get a report against the agreed metrics, written for finance as much as for engineering.
