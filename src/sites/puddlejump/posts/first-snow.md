The forecast said a dusting. We got four inches, a lot of very excited children, and one very lost mitten.

## Why we always go out

Cold days are when children notice the most: their breath, the crunch underfoot, how a footprint fills in. So we bundled up and spent the whole morning outside.

## What the Fireflies figured out

The Firefly Room spent an hour testing which things melt fastest on a warm hand. (Snow wins. Icicles are slower. Teachers' patience, it turns out, is endless.)

**Reminder:** please send snow pants, boots, and a spare pair of dry socks every day from now until April.
