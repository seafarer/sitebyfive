The mud kitchen is the busiest restaurant in Fort Collins. These recipes were dictated by the Meadow Room and written down word for word.

## Birthday cake (for a dinosaur)

"You need lots of mud and then you put sticks in for the candles. Then you sing. Then you don't eat it."

## Soup

"Water. Leaves. Stir it for a long, long time. Add a rock for crunch."

## Pancakes

"Flat mud. Pat pat pat. Flip it with the big spoon. It breaks every time, that's normal."

We'll be making real pancakes next month. Probably with fewer rocks.
