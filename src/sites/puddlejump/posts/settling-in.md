Every September, the Acorn Room fills with children who have never been to school before. Some walk in without looking back. Some need a few weeks. Both are completely normal.

## What we do

- **Keep goodbyes short and warm.** A hug, a clear "I'll be back after lunch," and out the door.
- **Offer a job.** Feeding the fish or watering a plant gives a child something to hold onto.
- **Send a photo.** If a child had a tricky goodbye, we text a photo once they've settled.

## What helps at home

Talk about school before it happens, and keep mornings predictable. And trust that tears at the door rarely last longer than the walk back to your car.
