---
title: Leaving for the winter? Five things to do before you go
description: A short list for owners closing up the house themselves.
date: 2025-10-20
author: Jesse Romero
---

Most of our owners leave the closing to us, but some like to do it themselves. Here's what we'd ask you to do before you lock the door.

1. **Set the heat no lower than 55°F.** Lower saves very little and risks frozen pipes on the north walls.
2. **Shut off the water to the washing machine** and any ice maker.
3. **Leave cabinet doors open** under sinks on exterior walls.
4. **Unplug what doesn't need power**, but leave the sump pump, heat tape, and alarm system on.
5. **Tell us your dates** so we can schedule the first walk-through within a week.
