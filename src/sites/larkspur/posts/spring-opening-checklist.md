Spring in the valley arrives in fits: sixty degrees on Tuesday, a foot of snow on Thursday. That's exactly when small winter problems become expensive ones.

## Water first

We turn water on slowly, one zone at a time, and walk every room while it pressurizes. Hose bibs and irrigation stay off until nights are reliably above freezing.

## Roof and gutters

Ice dams leave quiet damage. We check ceilings under every valley in the roof and clear gutters as soon as they're free of ice.

## The valve everyone forgets

If your home has a boiler, the fill valve can stick after a winter of sitting still. We test it on every spring visit, because a boiler without water is a very costly lesson.
