December has a way of compressing a year's worth of decisions into three weeks. A little sequencing helps.

## Worth doing before December 31

1. **Harvest losses deliberately.** Only where it doesn't distort the portfolio you actually want.
2. **Make charitable gifts from appreciated securities**, not cash, where your giving allows.
3. **Consider a partial Roth conversion** if this year's income is unusually low.
4. **Take required distributions** if you're subject to them.
5. **Review beneficiary designations.** They override your will, and they're often out of date.

## Fine to leave until spring

- Rebalancing that isn't tax-motivated
- Funding next year's retirement accounts
- Revisiting your insurance coverage

None of this is advice for your situation. It's a starting list for a conversation with your adviser and accountant.
