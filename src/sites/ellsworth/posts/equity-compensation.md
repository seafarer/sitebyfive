Most of the founders and executives we work with don't have a tax problem. They have a concentration problem that happens to have tax consequences.

## Decide the rule before the price moves

The hardest time to diversify is right after a big run-up, when selling feels like leaving money on the table. So we agree on a **written selling rule** while things are calm: a percentage of each vest, or a target concentration, with dates attached.

## Taxes matter, but they shouldn't drive

A rule that saves 5% in taxes but leaves half your net worth in one company is a bad trade. We model both, then let you choose with eyes open.
