---

Being named a trustee is an honor and a job. The first year sets the tone.

## Months one to three

Gather the trust document, an inventory of assets, and the contact details of every beneficiary. Open a trust account with a custodian if one doesn't exist.

## Months three to six

Write, or update, an **investment policy statement** that reflects the trust's purpose, not just its size. Communicate it to beneficiaries in plain language.

## The rest of the year

Keep records of every decision and the reasons for it. Good documentation is the trustee's best protection, and the beneficiaries' best evidence that the trust is working as intended.
