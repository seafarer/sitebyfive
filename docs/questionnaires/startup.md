# Startup website questionnaire

These questions give us everything we need to write your site. Short answers are fine; bullet points
are fine. If you already have a brochure, an old website, or a document that covers some of this,
email it to hello@sitebyfive.com and skip what it answers. Photos, your logo, and documents come in the next step, so for now just tell us what you have. Anything you leave blank shows up in your preview as a
clearly marked "add this later" note, never as made-up filler.
