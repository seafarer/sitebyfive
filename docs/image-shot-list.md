# Image shot list: new demo sites

Every image the four new demos reference, with the subject from its alt text (the alt text in the YAML stays as written).
File names and folders must match exactly. Until a file exists, its page shows a "missing image" placeholder, and the
demo stays `status: preview`.

**Style for every prompt** (prepend to each subject):

> Natural documentary photograph, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens, soft natural light, western Colorado
> high-desert and mountain setting, realistic colors, no text, no logos, no watermarks. People look natural and are not
> posing for or looking into the camera (except portraits, which are relaxed, eye-level, and shallow depth of field).

Export JPG, quality ~85. Sizes are targets; the build makes the smaller versions.
